TOTAL LAYERS: 11
CRITICAL LAYERS: 6 (if any fails, entire request fails)
HIGH PRIORITY: 3 (if misconfigured, requests slow down)
MEDIUM PRIORITY: 2 (if not optimized, costs increase)
